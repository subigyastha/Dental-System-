Three directions for our clinic

Recommended

A. Summary + tabs

Compact client details stay at the top. Summary is the default; full histories sit in named tabs.

Shortest default view. One extra click to read a full history.

B. Summary + collapsible sections

Keep one page. Expand Follow-up history, Payments or Visits when needed.

Familiar and easy to build. Opening several sections makes the page long again.

C. Client sidebar + timeline

Keep client details on the left and work/history on the right.

Useful on wide monitors. Less suitable for the phone-first clinic flow.

Recommended: client details first, one clear next action

What this removes

  • Repeated phone number and client code.
  • Large empty payment and medical sections.
  • Full visit and contact histories competing on the default screen.
  • Merge duplicate as the most prominent header action.

What stays obvious

  • Who the client is.
  • Whether a follow-up needs attention.
  • When we last spoke and the recorded result.
  • Log or schedule without leaving the client.

On the current historical client, “No upcoming follow-up” sits above an earlier reminder. The proposed summary leads with that due reminder and offers Reschedule for that same task.

Clinical alerts stay visible when present. “Not recorded” never means a confirmed absence. Multiple open reminders remain accessible, rather than being silently replaced.
